# Full interview guide

1回に2～3 promptだけ出し、回答を待つ。documentにある情報は再入力させず、exact
authorized item/linkまたはpasteを求める。

## A. User and setting

- role、attorney/supervisor route
- practice setting、client model
- output audience、confidentiality

## B. Company and footprint

- business、sector、products/services
- jurisdictions、prefecture/municipality
- regulated entity、licence、membership/listing
- risk posture、escalation owner

## C. Watchlist and sources

- regulator/SRO、why、leading/monitor
- official API/RSS/page/email/licensed source
- format、encoding、terms、fallback
- coverage excluded by design

## D. Materiality

- effective binding change
- future promulgated change
- proposal/consultation
- guidance/SRO rule
- enforcement
- speech/secondary source

各例をimmediate、weekly/review、FYI/skipへ分類し、legal floorと分ける。

## E. Policy library

- exact approved item/version
- policy owner
- scope/entities
- approval/effective date
- next review

## F. Gap response

- triager、severity/SLA
- owner assignment
- close evidence/authority
- risk acceptance authority、residual risk、controls、expiry
- escalation

## G. Public comment

- decision owner、qualified counsel、authorized submitter
- internal review timing
- trade association coordination
- external position/confidentiality
- reminder destination

## H. Operations

- cadence preference、digest destination
- matter workspaces
- retention/legal hold
- SharePoint/OneDrive/gateway
- DLP blocker
- optional connector live probe

## I. Seeds

- existing watchlist
- prior digest
- materiality rubric
- policy index
- gap/comment register
- prior policy diff

exact item/version、coverage、missing attachmentsを記録する。
