# Product legal full interview guide

1 turnに2～3 answerable promptまで。文書にありそうな情報はexact SharePoint itemを
先に求める。

## 1. User and practice

- role、attorney/supervisor route
- practice setting
- outputs/destination
- matter isolation

## 2. Company and product

- product、customer、business model、channel
- stage、listed/reporting status
- sectors/regulators
- jurisdictions、users/data/operations
- risk postureとcategory-specific deviation

## 3. Launch process

- intake source
- lead time
- formal gate/advisory
- output format
- PM/product/security/privacy/marketing/IR owner
- escalation trigger

## 4. Framework

- existing categories
- auto-skip facts
- Japan overlays
- foreign overlays
- evidence required

## 5. Calibration

各categoryについて:

- actual blocker example
- work-but-ships example
- FYI example
- novel/unseen pattern
- owner、timeline、resolution

## 6. Marketing/commerce

- comparative/absolute/AI/security claim posture
- evidence standard
- visual review
- influencer/review/stealth
- subscription/final screen
- platform policy

## 7. Japan/sector

- APPI/telecom
- consumer/claims
- product safety/platform
- minors/accessibility
- payments/finance/medical
- cyber/AI/IP
- public disclosure/MNPI

## 8. Seed reviews

past legal reviewを原則10件。PRDだけをseedにしない。read coverage、source version、
actual call、conditions、later learningを抽出する。

## 9. Storage/security

- exact libraries/lists
- state gateway
- ACL、retention、legal hold、DLP
- current-user draft
- authorized viewers

## 10. Watcher preference candidate

- desired horizon/cadence
- exact tracker scope
- trigger categories
- proposed destination/viewer

これはpreferenceであり、agent/flow/schedule/delivery evidenceではない。
