# Deal team summary format

```markdown
> **⚠️ レビュー担当者向け注記**
> - Sources:
> - Read:
> - Law / listing / guidance / contract / policy:
> - Flagged:
> - Currency:
> - Destination:
> - Before relying:

# [Deal code] — Diligence brief — [date]

**Audience:** [board-exec / deal-lead / working-team]
**Transaction structure:** [canonical value]
**Status:** [on track / issues identified / material blockers / unknown]
**Coverage:** [N/M documents; categories; unread]
**Summary:** [🔴 N · 🟠 N · 🟡 N · 🟢 N]

## Bottom line

[2–4 sentences]

## Material findings

1. [finding ID/title] — [deal impact] — [source] — [action]

## Regulatory / approval strip

| Screen | Status | Deadline/wait | Evidence | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Decisions needed

| Decision | Options | Owner | Due | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Since last brief

- New:
- Resolved:
- Severity/status change:
- Coverage:

## Gaps

- [missing document/fact/source]

## Next 72 hours

- [work item]
```

board-exec版は3–5 findingまで。working-team版はexact finding IDsへのtraceabilityを
落とさない。
