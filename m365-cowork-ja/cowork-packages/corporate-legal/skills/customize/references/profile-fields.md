# Corporate profile fields

## Company

- organization/name/industry/size
- practice setting
- jurisdictions/regulators/licences
- risk posture
- default escalation

## User

- role
- attorney contact/supervision
- approval authority

## Japan governance

- entity form
- registered head office/corporate number
- organ design
- articles/board regulation
- public-notice method
- share certificate/restricted shares
- Art. 370 authorization
- board/auditor/committee composition
- electronic records/signatures

## M&A

- side/structure/deal cadence/lead
- request list/materiality
- VDR/issues memo
- Luminance/Kira/trust/security
- closing/integration owner

## Board

- meeting cadence/portal
- minutes format/depth/turnaround
- consent mechanism/precedent
- resolution/recital/authorization language
- signature/evidence/retention

## Public

- exchange/market/EDINET
- annual/half-year/internal-control/extraordinary reports
- TDnet/governance report
- Arts. 163/166/167
- large holding/tender offer
- disclosure committee

## Entity / workflow

- entity table/ownership/control
- event/periodic obligation owner
- tax/social/labour/licence
- matter workspace/clean-team
- checklist/integration settings
- output/destination/dashboard
- gateway/connector/DLP/retention
