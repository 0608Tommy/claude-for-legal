# Japan hiring review checklist

- employing entity、establishment、work location/prefecture
- role、actual duties、change scope
- indefinite/fixed/part-time/dispatch等のcontract type
- term、renewal criteria/cap、indefinite-conversion notice
- start、probation、hours/system、break/holiday、36 agreement
- wages、allowances、fixed overtime、pay day、minimum wage
- retirement/dismissal/work rules/CBA
- accurate recruitment disclosure
- sex/pregnancy/age/disability、reasonable accommodation
- applicant data、background check、vendor/foreign transfer
- restrictive covenant、confidentiality、Patent Act Article 35
- visa/status、foreign-worker notification
- social/labor insurance onboarding owner
- 2026-10-01 future-law status

outputは`Clear to send`ではなく
`draft ready for qualified review | changes required | blocked/escalate`を使います。
