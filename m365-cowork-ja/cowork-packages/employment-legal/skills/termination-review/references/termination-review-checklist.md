# Japan termination review checklist

## Classification

- indefinite dismissal
- disciplinary action/dismissal
- fixed-term midterm dismissal
- nonrenewal
- retirement/resignation
- mutual separation/retirement solicitation
- workforce reduction

## Validity・procedure

- LCA Articles 15/16/17/18/19 as applicable
- LSA Articles 19/20/22/23
- work-rule/CBA basis、notice、fact finding、defense opportunity
- proportionality、comparators、prior warnings、consistent explanation
- occupational injury、maternity、leave、disability、whistleblowing、union
- current dismissal/RIF/nonrenewal case law

## RIF

- business necessity
- avoidance measures
- selection criteria/application
- explanation/consultation
- mass separation/reemployment-plan notice

## Offboarding

- final undisputed money/property after request
- retirement allowance source
- employment certificate/separation documents
- social/labor insurance loss
- foreign-worker notice
- property/access owner
- legal hold/retention

AIはgo/no-go、discipline、termination、access termination、filingを決定・実行しません。
