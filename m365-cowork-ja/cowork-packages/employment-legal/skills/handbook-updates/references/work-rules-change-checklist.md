# Handbook / work-rules change checklist

- exact current/proposed version、section、effective date
- `就業規則`該当性
- mandatory termへの影響
- 10+ establishment、majority union/representative opinion、filing、dissemination
- disadvantageous change and LCA Article 10
- CBA、36 agreement、other labor-management agreement
- cross-reference、defined term、form、system、training
- establishment/prefecture annex
- benefit/promise、transition、grandfathering
- harassment、leave、disability、whistleblower、monitoring、contractor safety
- source/effective date、future-law readiness
- version archive、acknowledgment/communication plan

AIはpublish、file、employee communication、acknowledgment collectionを実行しません。
