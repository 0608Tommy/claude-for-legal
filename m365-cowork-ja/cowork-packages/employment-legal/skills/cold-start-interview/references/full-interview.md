# Employment full interview

1 turnに2～3 answerable promptまで。documentにある情報はexact SharePoint item/linkを
先に求め、silent skipしません。

## 1. User・practice・security

- role:
  `Lawyer / legal professional | Non-lawyer with attorney access |
  Non-lawyer without regular attorney access`
- practice setting:
  `Solo / small firm | Midsize / large firm | In-house |
  Government / legal aid / clinic | Other`
- attorney/escalation route
- risk posture
- destination、retention、legal hold、Cowork DLP requirement

## 2. Japan footprint

- employing entity、registered address
- each establishment、prefecture、regular headcount
- remote/hybrid、employee residence/work location
- first hire/new establishment plan
- employees outside Japan
- union/CBA、majority representative

## 3. Employment foundation

- work rules version、filing/notice evidence、10+ establishment
- 36 agreement、special clause、term、covered employees
- working-time systems、objective time records
- wage/payroll、minimum-wage owner
- disability employment rate owner/current count
- gender information-publication owner
- harassment/whistleblower systems

## 4. Hiring・termination・classification

- legal review triggers
- employment-condition/contract templates
- fixed-term renewal/nonrenewal process
- background check and applicant data
- restrictive covenant/IP policy
- high-risk termination flags
- severance/release practice
- discipline/work-rule matrix
- contractor, freelancer, dispatch, EOR policy

## 5. Leave・accommodation・health

- HRIS/leave source and legal read access
- childcare/family-care/maternity/annual leave workflows
- insurance benefit owner
- disability/accommodation process
- alert windows as internal control
- restricted matter access groups

## 6. Investigation・whistleblowing

- intake channel、appointed handlers
- investigator/counsel roles
- interview notice
- internal evidentiary standard
- whistleblower identity separation
- memo and audience-summary patterns

## 7. International・insurance・immigration

- health/pension/employment/workers compensation owners
- foreign-worker status/notification owner
- secondment/EOR/dispatch arrangements
- outside counsel and tax route
- countries planned

## 8. Seed documents

- current handbook/work rules and filing/notice evidence
- 36 agreement/CBA
- offer/employment-condition templates
- recent termination/discipline/nonrenewal memos
- leave/accommodation procedure
- investigation protocol/memo
- contractor/EOR/dispatch template

各sourceにitem ID、version、date、coverage、viewer、confidentialityを記録します。

## 9. Future-law readiness

- 2026-10-01 harassment/social-insurance readiness
- 2026-12-01 whistleblower readiness
- 2027 immigration reform relevance
- owner、evidence、review date

future lawをcurrent settingへ保存しません。
